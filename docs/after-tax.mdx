The owner lives in Israel and pays Israeli tax. An arm that beats VT before tax can still lose to it after tax,
because an actively traded fund pays tax on its gains every year, while VT held pays once, at the end. Since
2 Oct 2026 the experiment checks that, and shows every result after tax and in shekels too.

## The after-tax gate

Pre-registration section 5c. The arm that would win must also beat the VT fund **after Israeli tax**, on an
"if sold today" basis, at the same bar and with the same Newey-West test as the fund test (lag 5).

- **After-tax equity** = the fund's equity at the close, minus the tax due if every position were sold at that
  close. A year's tax is never below 0.
- **After-tax daily return** = after-tax equity today ÷ after-tax equity yesterday − 1.
- **The test** = the arm's fund minus the VT fund, day by day, from the first fund session (29 Sep 2026) to the
  close of the look's last trades.

The funds run makes the test once, on the first night from the night the race reaches a look on which the race can
read the look, the rate table is there and the funds' prices reach the look's last close, and keeps it. The race
reads it back the next night. If a bug fix later moves the look's window, the test is made again at the new close. It can only stop an arm from winning; it never decides anything by itself.

## The tax rules

Every number is in `config/israel_tax.py`; the arithmetic is in `analysis/israel_tax.py`, with the owner's ten
cases as tests (`tests/test_israel_tax.py`).

| Rule | What the code does |
| --- | --- |
| Gains in shekels | cost and proceeds in shekels at the day's rate; the tax is on the smaller of the shekel gain and the dollar gain at the sale-day rate; a loss made only by the exchange rate is not deductible |
| Rate | 25% on real gains and on dividends |
| Surtax | 3% + 2% above 721,560 ILS, as parameters; off |
| Lots | FIFO, per account; a reinvested dividend is a new lot |
| Dividends | 25% Israeli tax, with a credit for the 25% US tax withheld (W-8BEN); the extra is usually 0 |
| Losses | first against the same year's gains; the rest against the year's dividends (switch on, until the accountant answers), then carried forward against future gains only, with no expiry |
| Exchange rate | the Bank of Israel's representative rate on the trade date |

The open questions are in `docs/research/cpa-questions.md`. The answers will change settings only through a
dated amendment.

## The shekel view

The same results in shekels, at the Bank of Israel's representative rate for each day (`analysis/boi_rates.py`).
For a day the Bank did not publish, the European Central Bank's reference rates crossed through the euro are
used, and if neither has the day, the Bank's last earlier rate; every such day is counted and listed.

The view shows how much of each shekel result came from the dollar's move against the shekel: the shekel
return minus the dollar return. If the dollar fell 3% against the shekel, a fund that made 2% in dollars lost
about 1% in shekels, and the view says the dollar's move took about 3 points. It is shown before tax and after
tax ("if sold today").

The rate table is kept like the price table: its SHA-256 (`rates_sha256`) is committed in `logs/fx_rates.json`
and `logs/funds.json`, and the table itself goes to the archive.

Each night's result is in `logs/after_tax.md` and the funds job's summary: whether the table was written, how many
days came from the Bank of Israel and how many from another source. A night with no table, or with no day from the
Bank of Israel at all, and a night the funds run itself fails, are sent to the phone (`scoring-prices.yml`, because
the funds run holds no secret). On a pull request that changes the rate code, the funds smoke test fetches the rates
from a GitHub runner too, as a reachability check.

## Where to read it

- **The 4 Funds page**, "After tax and in shekels": the paper account every night; the four funds once
  calibration has passed.
- **`logs/after_tax.md`**: the same, as a short report.
- **`logs/funds.json`**, key `after_tax`: everything, with each look's after-tax test under `after_tax.looks`.
- **The exploratory funds** after tax: only in the checkpoint records, at the race's looks.

Before and after tax are side by side, on two bases, in dollars and in shekels: **tax paid so far** (equity minus
the tax on everything sold or received so far, as if the year ended today) and **if sold today** (equity minus that
tax plus the tax on every open position sold at the close). Both tax amounts are shown too, in shekels.

## For information: the break-even

`python -m analysis.tax_breakeven --table`: how much more a year, before tax, an actively traded fund must earn to
tie with VT held for 20 years. At 6% growth and a 2% dividend yield it is about 0.82 points a year. It is not a
gate.

## What is not modeled

- Alpaca's paper record has no dividends, so none are taxed for the paper account.
- The funds pay no tax during the year; the after-tax equity takes the tax off as if it were paid when due, so it
  does not shrink the money the fund trades with.
- "If sold today" sells at the close with no selling cost.
- Business-income treatment of frequent trading is not modeled (it is not among the owner's questions to the accountant of 3 Oct 2026).
