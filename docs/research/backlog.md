# Research backlog

Ideas that might become exploratory tests one day. **Updated monthly** (the
first working day of each month). Nothing here runs: an idea runs only after
it has a card in `cards/`, a row in `graveyard.md`, and a registration in the
pre-registration (section 13).

## The limits (pre-registration, section 13.8)

- A, B and C are the starting set. **No other new exploratory test before
  the first checkpoint (2026-12-22).**
- **From 2027-01-01: at most 2 new ideas per quarter, registered only at
  checkpoints** (the race's looks).
- Every idea tried, including one dropped before it runs, gets a row in
  `graveyard.md`, and the count there is N for the Deflated Sharpe Ratio.
  So every idea added makes every other result a little harder to believe.

## How to add an idea

One row below: the idea in one sentence, its published source, what it
would be compared with, and the date it was added. When it is picked at a
checkpoint, write its card from `cards/_template.md`, add its graveyard row,
then register it.
