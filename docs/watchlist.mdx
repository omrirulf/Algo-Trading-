The watchlist lives in `config/watchlist.py`, not in an environment variable, so
a change to what gets traded shows up in a diff — the same reasoning that keeps
the guardrail thresholds in code.

```python
from config.watchlist import DEFAULT_WATCHLIST, BUCKETS
```

| Bucket | Count | Tickers |
|---|---|---|
| US sectors | 10 | MSFT, NVDA, GOOGL, JPM, UNH, LLY, PG, COST, CAT, NEE |
| Europe (ADR) | 6 | ASML, SAP, NVO, SHEL, AZN, UL |
| Israel | 6 | TEVA, CHKP, NICE, CYBR, MNDY, ESLT |
| Emerging (ADR) | 4 | TSM, INFY, HDB, VALE |
| Commodity producers | 4 | XOM, NEM, FCX, NTR |

## Why not AAPL, MSFT, NVDA

That was the default from the first commit, and it was a placeholder rather than
a choice. Three US mega-cap technology names move together closely enough that
they are nearer **one position than three** — the opposite of what a watchlist
is for. Breadth across sector, geography and asset driver is the point.

## Why individual equities, not ETFs

The obvious way to reach Europe or gold is `VGK` and `GLD`. It does not work
here, and the reason is structural rather than taste.

This pipeline builds a **five-dimension** prompt: news, technicals,
fundamentals, the analyst view, and insider filings. An ETF has no earnings, no
margins, no price targets and no Form 4 filings, and a Google News query for its
ticker returns almost nothing.

<Warning>
  **Four of the five dimensions come back empty for an ETF.** The prompt names
  them as gaps and the model is told to score them `0.0`, so what actually runs
  is a technicals-only strategy that nothing here has tested — exactly the
  failure mode the [news-skip rule](/orchestrator/news) exists to prevent.
</Warning>

So geography and commodity exposure come through *companies* that happen to be
US-listed: ADRs for Europe and Asia, US-listed Israeli issuers, and **producers**
rather than the commodity itself. A gold miner has earnings, analysts and
insiders; `GLD` has none of them. The exposure is less pure — a miner carries
company risk the metal does not — and that is the price of keeping the strategy
the one that was actually built.

<Note>
  One partial exception: insiders at foreign private issuers generally do not
  file Form 4, so the insider dimension will usually be an honest gap for the
  ADRs. That degrades one dimension of five, not four — and it shows up as a
  named gap rather than a silent zero.
</Note>

## What this cannot reach

Alpaca trades `us_equity`, `us_option` and `crypto` only — confirmed from the
SDK's own `AssetClass` enum. There is **no path** to the Frankfurt, Paris,
London or Tel Aviv order books, and none to futures, so physical oil and gold
are out of reach whatever the watchlist says. Everything above is US-listed.

## Cost scales with length

One Bright Data request and one Claude call per ticker per cycle. Input is
measured from the real prompt (~1,400 tokens); output is **assumed** at ~1,600
tokens, priced at Opus 5's list rate:

| Watchlist size | Rough Claude spend / month |
|---|---|
| 5 | ~$35 |
| 15 | ~$104 |
| 30 (default) | ~$207 |

<Warning>
  **These are estimates, and the assumed output length is the whole
  uncertainty in them.** Output dominates the bill, so guessing it wrong moves
  the total proportionally. Once you have run real cycles the journal carries
  measured `usage` blocks — use those instead of this table, and see
  [Cost](/cost) for how to read them and how to compare configurations.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Assumes ~7 cycles per trading day (what the market-hours guard leaves) over 21
  trading days, plus Bright Data on top.
  `config.watchlist.estimated_monthly_cost_usd()` is the same arithmetic if you
  want to try a different length.
</Note>

Trimming the list is one lever, but not the first one: **effort is cheaper to
turn than length**, because thinking bills at the output rate. See
[Cost](/cost). `HEARTBEAT_INTERVAL_MINUTES` is the third.

<Warning>
  **A longer list does not mean more positions.** `MAX_OPEN_POSITIONS` still
  caps holdings at 10 and `MAX_POSITION_PCT` at 5% each. Breadth buys the model
  more to choose between, not more exposure — see [Guardrails](/guardrails).
</Warning>

## Overriding it

`WATCHLIST` still works and takes precedence, for a quick experiment:

```bash
WATCHLIST=MSFT,ASML python -m orchestrator.heartbeat --once
```

For a lasting change, edit `config/watchlist.py` so it lands in a commit.
